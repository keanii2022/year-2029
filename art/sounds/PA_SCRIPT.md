# Store speaker script

Read these into GarageBand as one take, like you did the callouts. Leave about two seconds of silence between lines and say the number before each line ("one", pause, the line). Claude cuts them up and turns them into the store speakers: tinny, echoing through the warehouse.

How to sound: a store announcer who is calm, slow and way too polite. Smile while you talk. At night, get flatter and slower.

## Day
1. Good morning, shoppers! The store is open. Have a wonderful day!
2. Attention shoppers: the store will never close.
3. A reminder that smiling is mandatory in aisle 40.
4. Today's special is everything, forever.
5. Lost children will collect themselves at the front desk.
6. Please do not look directly at the helpers.
7. Standing still is shoplifting. Keep moving.
8. We are always happy to help. Always.
9. Please do not go into aisle 12.
10. Thank you for shopping. Thank you. Thank you.
11. The exit is somewhere else.
12. If you hear your name, do not answer.
13. Fresh bread in the bakery. There is no bakery.

## Siren
14. Lights out in ten. Nine. Eight. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Three. Two. One.
15. The store is now closed. Please stay where our helpers can find you.

## Night (slower, flatter)
16. Attention shoppers. The store is now closed. Please remain where you are.
17. Shopper in aisle thirty two, please remain still.
18. Shopper in aisle sixty four, please remain still.
19. Shopper in aisle nine, please remain still.
20. Will the parents of... (one second of silence) ...please come to the front desk.
21. Thank you for staying with us. Thank you for staying. Thank you.
