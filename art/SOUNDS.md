# What everything sounds like

Every sound has a loudness. Loudness decides who hears it, and that is half the game: noise is a choice.

| Loudness | Players hear it within | Robots hear it within | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| quiet | 20 studs | only inside their own room | small clicks, sips, pages |
| normal | 45 studs | 25 studs | doors, drawers, eating, gear |
| loud | 120 studs | 80 studs, and they come look | alarms, music, crashes, guns |

Rules
- Sound is 3D: it comes from the object, gets muffled through walls and forts.
- Robots walk to the last loud sound they heard. Throwing a noise maker or setting off a TV is a real move.
- Sources: Roblox's own free sound library, or sounds we make ourselves. Nothing ripped from other games or shows, no real songs.
- Volumes and distances live in Config so playtests can tune them.

## Music
Keani's own track, made in GarageBand, cut into four loops in `sounds/music/`. One plays at a time, crossfading over about 2 seconds when the moment changes.
| Loop | When | Loudness |
|---|---|---|
| music_day_loop | lights on: rotates with music_dawn_loop, one after the other | quiet bed under everything |
| music_dawn_loop | lights on: rotates with music_day_loop | quiet bed |
| music_night_loop | night (lights out), the only night song | quiet bed |
| music_installation_loop | a player is close to an installation; overrides whatever is playing while they're near | normal |
